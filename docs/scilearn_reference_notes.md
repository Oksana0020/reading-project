# Reference Platform Notes: Learner Experience Patterns

**Review date:** 2026-09-07  
**Scope:** Reference-only product review of the user-authorised learner interface. No external records were created, edited, downloaded, or retained.

## Observed patterns

The learner interface separates work into three clear destinations: **My Assignments**, **Demos**, and **Resources**. Its assignment view uses a simple “Choose an assignment” entry point. The demo area groups short practice activities by increasing reading level and makes clear that demo points/progress are not counted. The resources area contains printable reading-strategy charts, student checklists, completion/streak charts, keyboard-shortcut guides, activity-specific help sheets, and short introductory/tutorial videos.

The assignment page also makes the learner's next destination unambiguous, separates assigned work from Demos and Resources in the top navigation, and provides concise recovery guidance when an assignment launch or browser state is not ready. These patterns support Reader Leader additions such as a focused warm-up before an assigned story, clear live-reading readiness feedback, and a child-facing non-assessed practice area. No learner names, assignments, performance data, or account content from the reference platform are retained in this document.

The Demos view exposes programme/level libraries rather than three in-session error-handling modes. The visible libraries include Fast ForWord Foundations I and II, Elements I and II, Reading Readiness, Reading Levels 1–3, and Reading Comprehension, with named activities inside each. The active assignment page displayed a ClearFluency library. These programme choices should not be described as equivalent to Reader Leader’s Assisted, Guided, and Monthly modes; they organise activities, while Reader Leader’s modes govern child feedback and progression within one oral-reading session. Demo progress is explicitly not saved or counted.

## Transferable Reader Leader opportunities

| Observed pattern | Practical Reader Leader adaptation | Safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Separate assignment, practice, and resource destinations | Add a child-facing **Practice Lab** and a small **Reading Toolkit** alongside the existing assigned-story library. | Keep teacher-assigned work clearly separate from optional practice so dashboards are not misleading. |
| Levelled activity catalogue | Offer opt-in, teacher-curated short fluency drills organised by reading level and skill focus. | Do not auto-assign or infer a child’s ability from speech results. |
| “Demo progress does not count” explanation | Label optional practice as **Try it out — not part of your reading report**. | Exclude it from WCPM, story-match, and monthly-assessment metrics. |
| Strategy sheets, checklists, and how-to material | Add printable before/during/after reading prompts, a child checklist, and brief parent/teacher guides. | Use age-appropriate, accessible language and keep family data out of generic resources. |
| Completion and streak charts | Extend the existing supportive progress trail with weekly goals and gentle completion indicators. | Make goals teacher/parent-configurable and avoid punitive streak loss mechanics. |
| Activity-specific tutorial help | Add a short “How to read with the coach” guide plus microphone, connection, and playback troubleshooting. | Explain that browser live recognition can be unavailable and is not diagnostic. |

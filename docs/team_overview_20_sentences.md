# Reader Leader Team Overview — 20 Simple Sentences

1. I built **Reader Leader**, a role-aware reading application for children aged 8–10.
2. The app gives children, teachers, and parents separate spaces with the right information for each role.
3. Children can choose a story, read aloud, and receive kind, age-appropriate feedback.
4. The reading canvas tracks words as unread, current, correct, incorrect, or self-corrected.
5. Assisted Practice highlights reading progress and supports a child who wants to try a word again.
6. Guided Practice plays a model word after two unsuccessful attempts and then allows another try.
7. Monthly Assessment records a quiet first pass without interrupting the child with correction prompts.
8. I used the browser speech-recognition capability to provide a live transcript preview while a child reads.
9. I used the ASR transcription service to turn a saved authorised recording into a server-side transcript after the session.
10. The ASR flow also produces timed segments that support approximate word-linked playback for teacher review.
11. A teacher can click a word in a saved transcript and listen to the matching short audio moment.
12. The ASR output is a supportive prototype signal and is not presented as a diagnostic judgement of a child’s speech.
13. Teachers can upload PDF, DOCX, or text materials and review extracted reading text.
14. The app creates structured vocabulary and comprehension exercise drafts that teachers review before assigning.
15. Teachers can organise several classes, add learners, and set a default reading mode and target WCPM for each learner.
16. Teacher dashboards show monthly assessment story match and WCPM trends for all classes or one selected class.
17. Teachers can download the same monthly trend data as a CSV file for external reporting.
18. Parents see simple progress, celebratory reading badges, and a three-step home-practice checklist.
19. Completing the checklist creates one private in-app reminder for the linked parent and prevents same-day duplicates.
20. The system saves authorised sessions, reports, feedback, quiz attempts, checklist activity, and role-scoped data in the application database.
